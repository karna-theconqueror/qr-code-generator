## FAQ

**Q: Can I change the destination URL after printing?**
A: Yes! All QR codes use dynamic links. Change the destination anytime in the dashboard without reprinting.

**Q: What analytics do you provide?**
A: Total scans, today's scans, device type (mobile/desktop/tablet), browser, operating system, 7-day trend chart, and recent scan history with timestamps.

**Q: Do the QR codes expire?**
A: No. They work forever. You own the QR codes and the dashboard access.

**Q: Can I track scans from specific locations?**
A: The dashboard shows device, browser, and OS. For IP-based location, that's available in the scan history table.

**Q: What formats do you deliver?**
A: High-resolution PNG (for print) and SVG (for scaling). Both at your custom size.

**Q: Can I add my logo to the QR code?**
A: Yes! Upload your logo and it will be centered on the QR code automatically.
